Frequently asked questions

What is bounding box annotation?

Bounding box annotation draws rectangles around objects in an image or video frame and tags each one with a class, such as person, vehicle or traffic light. The labeled boxes are used to train and test computer vision models.

How much does bounding box annotation cost?

Reference rates start at $0.025 per box on the Basic package and fall to $0.0185 per box on Premium and Enterprise. The first 100 items are free, and first-time users get a straight $500 cash discount on projects over $3,000.

How accurate is your annotation?

We target 98% or better accuracy using dual-layer QA on every batch. If a batch falls below your quality standard, we redo it at no cost.

Do you have a minimum project size?

No. You can start at any scale, from the free 100-item pilot to hundreds of thousands of boxes.

How fast can you deliver?

Datasets are typically delivered in 2 to 5 days depending on volume. Larger projects are scheduled up front, for example a 100,000-box project is planned over about 15 days.

Is our data kept private?

Yes. We sign an NDA before any data is shared, only our in-house team handles your data, workspaces are encrypted and stream-only, and your data is deleted after the project.
