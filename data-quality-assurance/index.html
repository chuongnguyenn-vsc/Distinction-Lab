Frequently asked questions

What is dual-layer QA?

Annotators label the data, then a second layer reviews every batch before delivery. In the free trial, items go through our full dual-annotator and lead QA pipeline.

What accuracy do you target?

We target 98% or better. If a batch falls below your quality standard, we redo it at no cost.

How are edge cases and disagreements handled?

Consensus review resolves disagreements between annotators, and your feedback is built back into the workflow so later batches are more consistent.

Can I test your quality before committing?

Yes. Send a small sample dataset and your labeling guidelines and we return finished data for free, with no commitment or credit card.

Is our data kept private?

Yes. We sign an NDA before any data is shared, only our in-house team handles your data, workspaces are encrypted and stream-only, and your data is deleted from our systems when the project ends.
