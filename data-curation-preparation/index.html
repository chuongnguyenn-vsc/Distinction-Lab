Frequently asked questions

What does data curation and preparation include?

It includes data cleaning (removing errors, duplicates and inconsistencies), data structuring, schema and guideline design, and data validation for completeness and consistency.

How is data preparation different from annotation?

Preparation gets raw data into a clean, well-structured state and defines the labeling rules. Annotation then applies labels such as bounding boxes or text tags. We can run both as one managed project.

How is data preparation priced?

Pricing is quoted per project based on volume, task complexity and turnaround. There are no per-seat licenses or annual lock-in. The first 100 items are free.

Do you have a minimum project size?

No. You can start at any scale, from the free 100-item pilot to very large datasets.

Is our data kept private?

Yes. We sign an NDA before any data is shared, only our in-house team handles your data, workspaces are encrypted and stream-only, and your data is deleted from our systems when the project ends.
