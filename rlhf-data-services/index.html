Frequently asked questions

What is RLHF data?

RLHF stands for reinforcement learning from human feedback. It uses human preference judgments, such as ranking which of two model responses is better, to train and align language models. RLHF data is the set of those human rankings, ratings and rewritten responses.

What types of LLM data do you provide?

We provide preference ranking, instruction and response data, and human review and rewriting of model outputs, all produced to your guidelines.

How is RLHF work priced?

It is quoted per project based on volume, task complexity and turnaround. The first 100 items are free, and first-time users get a straight $500 cash discount on projects over $3,000.

How do you keep rater quality consistent?

We run a free calibration batch to agree the guidelines, then apply dual-layer QA to every batch. Work that does not match your quality standard is redone free.

Can we start with a small test?

Yes. There is no minimum project size, and the free 100-item pilot lets you evaluate our quality before you commit.
