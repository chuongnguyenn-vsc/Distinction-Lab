Frequently asked questions

What is a managed data annotation team?

It is a dedicated team that we recruit, train and manage for your project. We handle setup, production, quality control and delivery, so you do not have to hire or manage annotators yourself.

How much does a managed team cost compared with hiring?

We estimate an in-house team at $8K to $15K+ per month, fully loaded, before a single item is labeled. Distinction Lab projects are priced per project and cost up to 70% less, with a quote before work begins.

Do you have a minimum project size?

No. We are built for startups and SMEs, so there is no high-volume enterprise minimum. The team scales up or down as your data needs change.

Do I work with the team directly?

Yes. A customer service officer and the annotators work directly with you throughout, like an in-house team.

How do I get started?

Send your guidelines and up to 100 sample items for the free calibration trial. You see our quality and turnaround before you commit.
