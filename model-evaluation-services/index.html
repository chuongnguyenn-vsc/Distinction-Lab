Frequently asked questions

What is human-led model evaluation?

It is expert human review of a model's outputs against a rubric you define, used to find where the model performs well, where it fails, and how versions compare.

What do we receive at the end?

Structured ratings and notes in the format you specify, so your team can analyse results and prioritise fixes.

How is evaluation priced?

Quoted per project based on volume, rubric complexity and turnaround. The first 100 items are free.

Can you evaluate both LLMs and computer vision models?

We review model outputs to your rubric across the data types we work with. Tell us your task and we will confirm fit in the free pilot.
