[ 02 ]Pipeline

How your data moves through the lab

One pipeline, four checkpoints. Every item is annotated twice, reconciled, and audited before it reaches your dataset.

Step 0 — Free Calibration Trial

Send us up to 100 items and your labeling schema. We’ll staff a pod, run the full pipeline below, and hand back finished data for free. Then you can evaluate our accuracy and turnaround before committing.

$0 · No commitment required
Step 1 Schema & Pod Setup

We convert your labeling guidelines into a production-ready schema and deploy a dedicated pod custom-trained for your specific domain whether Computer Vision, NLP, Audio, or Conversational RLHF.

Step 2 Dual-Annotator Pass

Every item is labeled independently by trained annotators. Disagreements are flagged automatically rather than silently averaged away.

Step 3 Lead QA Audit

A senior QA lead reconciles disagreements, spot-audits consensus items, and resolves edge cases against your guidelines before anything is marked done.

Step 4 Delivery & Iteration

Clean, ground-truth data lands in your pipeline on a cadence you set. Feedback loops back to the pod so quality compounds batch over batch.
