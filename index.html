How does the free 100-item trial work?+

Send us your schema and up to 100 sample items. We run them through our full dual-annotator + Lead QA pipeline and deliver labeled data back to you — free, with no commitment or credit card required.

How is Distinction Lab priced?+

Pricing is quoted per project based on volume, task complexity, and turnaround needs — not a flat per-seat SaaS fee. There are no hidden platform markups or high enterprise minimums, so cost scales with what you actually need.

How do you protect our data?+

All engagements are covered by NDA before we see a single sample. Access to your data is limited to the pod assigned to your project, and we can work within your security and compliance requirements.

What's your typical turnaround time?+

Turnaround depends on task complexity and volume, and is agreed upfront for every batch. Your trial batch will give you a real, task-specific benchmark before you commit to an ongoing cadence.

Do we need a minimum volume to work with you?+

No. We're built for startups and SMEs specifically, so there's no high-volume enterprise minimum. Pods scale up or down as your data needs change.
